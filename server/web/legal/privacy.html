Privacy Policy

verSHion shared question pool. Last updated: 2026-10-22 (draft).

In short: we collect the minimum needed to run an invite-only pool for teachers: your email, name, a password hash, sign-in session data and what you publish. We do not sell it, show ads, or track you with third-party scripts. There are no student accounts, and student data must never be uploaded.

1. Who is responsible

[OPERATOR NAME] (“we”) runs the pool as an individual. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]. This policy covers the pool only. The verSHion app keeps your banks and exams on your own device unless you choose to publish.

3. Who can see what

  • Other approved teachers see items you make public: the content as published, your display name, the licence, and the attribution chain. They never see your email or school.
  • Circle members see the banks their circle shares, with the publisher’s display name and the licence; the circle’s owner controls who joins, with a code. A bank shared to a circle is not public.
  • Hidden keys. If you publish with “hide key”, other teachers receive only a version without answers, explanations or figures; the full version is stored separately and served only to you. Items published without hide key show answers to every approved teacher who can see them.
  • Unlisted items (published but not made public) are visible only to you.
  • The administrator (the operator, and any admin the operator appoints) can see account details, the approval queue, reports, and the full version of any item, including hidden keys, in order to moderate. Admin actions are logged.
  • Your display name is copied into the copies other teachers import and their attribution chains. Those copies live on other people’s devices, and we cannot reach them.
  • Reports. A report goes to the administrator. The reporter is told only that the report will be reviewed.

4. No sale, no ads

We do not sell or rent personal information, do not show advertising, and do not use your content or data to train AI models. We share information only with the service providers below, or when the law requires it or to protect someone’s safety.

7. Deleting your account, and what remains

You can delete your account from the app (you re-enter your password). It stops working at once. You can also ask us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. Before deleting, you can export your own data: in the app, under account settings, use the Export button to download a JSON file with your account profile fields, your items and their publishes (versions), your imports list, the reports you filed, any notices you sent or received, and your publish attestations. Or email us and we will send it. [CONFIRM when shipped]

Removed immediately: your email, name, profile and password hash; all sessions and device tokens; free-text you wrote (report notes, appeal notes, independence statements); the name, address, phone and signature in any counter-notice you filed; and your preferences.

Your shared items: you can choose, before deleting, whether to keep them (shown as by “deleted teacher”) or have them removed. If you make no choice, items that other teachers have already imported are kept without your name, and the rest are hidden and purged after 14 days. Once purged they cannot be restored. A returning person who signs up again with the same email is a new account; the old items are not re-attached.

What remains:

  • A bare account record with no personal data, so identifiers stay consistent. A one-way hash of your email address is kept for 14 days and then deleted.
  • Copies other teachers already imported stay on their devices under the licence they were made with. We cannot recall them. After you delete your account, your name shows as “deleted teacher” on others’ copies and in their attribution chains (the change is applied in batches, so it may take a short while). The licence and credit to the original work still apply.
  • Pseudonymous records under an opaque account id: import counts (so counts do not drop), audit-log entries of security and moderation actions (with free-text notes removed), and content fingerprints (keyed hashes, not readable text) that record who published a question first, which is how we protect original authors and attribution.
  • Copyright records (notices, strikes) as required to keep a complete record. Counter-notice personal fields are removed, but ids and dates remain.
  • Backups, until they expire (see above).

8. Student data

The pool is built for questions, answers, explanations and figures only. Do not upload student personal information: names, grades, scores, work samples, photos, ID numbers, or anything that identifies a student. Educators must not upload education records or anything protected by student-privacy law such as FERPA. We do not need and do not want this data, and the app has no student fields.

If student data does appear:

  • Any approved teacher can report it. One report from an established account hides the item at once and alerts us.
  • If a publish matches text we have already confirmed to be student data, it is held for review, and the author is told it is “held for review”.
  • If we confirm it, the item is deleted immediately, matching images and text are blocked from being re-uploaded, and people who imported it are asked to delete their copies. The author can appeal for 14 days.
  • If we reject the report, the item is restored.

If you believe student data was published, report it in the app or email [CONTACT EMAIL].

9. Images

Images you publish are re-encoded on your device and checked again on our server. They must be a single still PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP (up to 2 MB and 4096 × 4096 pixels). Camera data, location data and comments are removed, and animated images keep only their first frame. Images are stored in private Cloudflare R2 storage and are served only to people allowed to see the item. We do not scan images for viruses.

10. Children

The pool is for adults who work in schools. There are no student accounts, and children may not sign up. If we learn that an account belongs to a child, we will delete it.

11. Security

Passwords are stored as Argon2id hashes, tokens and invite codes are stored hashed, sign-in is rate-limited, and nothing is cached in shared browsers. No online service is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information we will tell you as the law requires.
