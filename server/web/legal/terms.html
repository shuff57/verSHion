Hiding answer keys

You can publish with “hide key”. Other teachers then receive a version without correct answers, explanations or figures, and with answer order shuffled. We do our best to check this, but the check is automatic: you are responsible for reviewing the preview before you publish, because the question text, images or tags can still give an answer away. Items published without hide key show their answers to every approved teacher who can see them. Our administrator can view the full version of any item for moderation.

4. What you must not publish

  • Student personal data, such as names, grades, scores, work samples, photos, ID numbers or anything that identifies a student or reads as an education record.
  • Answer keys you did not mean to share, including answers hidden in question text, images, figures, tags or titles, and keys for material that belongs to someone else.
  • Copied published material you have no permission to share: textbook questions, publisher test banks, exam-board papers, paid worksheets.
  • Content that is unlawful, harassing, hateful, or inappropriate for a school setting.
  • Malicious files or attempts to break, overload or scrape the service, or to get around limits, rate limits or content checks.

The Acceptable Use & Community Guidelines explain these with examples. Images must be a single still PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, at most 2 MB and 4096 × 4096 pixels. Metadata such as camera data is stripped. Animated images are shared as their first frame only.

There are also usage limits (for example storage, number of shared questions, and daily caps). Current defaults: 200 MB of storage and 5,000 shared questions per account.
