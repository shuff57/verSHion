Acceptable Use & Community Guidelines

verSHion shared question pool. Last updated: 2026-10-22 (draft).

In short: share your own work, pick an honest licence, credit what you reuse, keep student data and leaked keys out, be kind, and report problems. These guidelines are part of the Terms of Service.

1. Share your own work

  • Publish only questions you wrote, or that you have the right to share under the licence you choose.
  • Do not copy from textbooks, publisher test banks, exam-board papers or paid worksheets without permission. Rewording a few words is still copying.
  • You confirm this each time you publish. Take that tick seriously.
  • If you have a legitimate reason to publish something similar to what is already there (a very common question, or something you wrote independently), the app lets you say so. Your statement is kept and may be reviewed.

2. Licences and attribution

  • Pick a licence on purpose. CC BY 4.0 is suggested, but nothing is chosen for you. Other options let you forbid commercial use or require share-alike.
  • When you import and then publish, the licence of what you started from limits what you may choose. If it conflicts, the app will say which items are the cause. Do not try to avoid it by deleting the origin information.
  • Keep credit. Attribution travels with each copy; do not remove or hide it, and say when you have changed something.

3. No student data

  • No student names, grades, scores, work, photos, IDs or anything that identifies a student, including in images, screenshots and examples. A pasted class roster or a scanned student answer is not acceptable.
  • If you spot student data in the pool, report it as “student data”. A single report hides the item at once while we check it.

4. Answer keys

  • The pool is only useful if students cannot read it. Use “hide key” when you do not want other teachers to see your answers, and read the preview of what others will see.
  • Do not publish answers to material you do not own, such as an exam board’s mark scheme.
  • Check for answers hidden in the question text, images, figures, titles and tags.
  • Do not share what you receive with students, and do not post pool content publicly where students can find it. If a hidden key you published has leaked, or you find one, use the “key leak” report.

5. Be decent

  • No harassment, hate, threats or bullying, including in names, titles, tags, notes and reports.
  • Content must suit a school setting.
  • Use your real name or a name colleagues will recognise as you. Do not pretend to be someone else.
  • Do not use the pool to advertise.

6. Don’t abuse the service

  • No scraping, bulk downloading or automated use. There are daily limits on publishing, importing and reading.
  • No attempts to get around rate limits, content checks, the approval process, or the privacy of hidden keys.
  • No fake or repeated reports, and no pre-publishing other people’s work to block them.
  • Do not share your account with anyone, especially students.

7. Reporting a problem

Use the “report” action on any item in the app. Reasons you can choose: wrong answer, copyright, student data, key leak, inappropriate, other. Only approved teachers who are not the author can report. Three or more separate teachers reporting an item (for reasons other than student data) hides it until we review it. Reports are reviewed by the administrator, and the reporter is told only that it will be reviewed. For a formal copyright claim, use the Copyright Policy.

8. What happens if the rules are broken

  • An item can be held for review, taken down or deleted. You can appeal from the app.
  • Copyright takedowns count as strikes only when upheld; repeat strikes lead to account review and possible suspension (see the Copyright Policy).
  • Serious or repeated problems can lead to suspension or removal of your account. Suspension hides your items from other people but does not delete them.
  • Where the law requires, or someone could be harmed, we may involve your school or the authorities.
