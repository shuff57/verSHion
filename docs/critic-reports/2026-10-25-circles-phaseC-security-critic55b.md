## Blockers (new class)

### B1. Refused answer-key PATCHes are free, so the CPU bound on PATCH hide_key is gone (round 47 54a B2 undid round 46's 20/day bound)
- `server/worker/src/pool/circles.ts:1454-1462` (`chargeHideChange` then `giveDaily` on every refusal), the strip it pays for: `hideOn` -> `stripStored` (`circlehide.ts:174`) under the heavy slot (`HEAVY_CONCURRENCY` 1, `publish.ts:894`).
- Exploit (`c55/h1.test.ts`): any circle owner (own circle) or bank author sends one 3.1 MB bank whose choices carry letter labels ("A. ..."), then repeats `PATCH {hide_key: true}` with no label decisions. Each answers `422 labels_need_decision` after a full parse and strip of the head, then the day's count is given back. Result: 30 refused PATCHes took 15.0 s (about 480 ms of heavy-slot work each), `usage_daily.circle_hide_changes` stayed 0 (the 20/day cap and its 429 are never reached); 12 parallel took 5.7 s; a victim's public publish sent while 12 were queued waited 1.0 s (the queue is FIFO; at the generic write rate of about 1 request a second one account holds roughly half to two thirds of the isolate's only heavy slot indefinitely, a few accounts hold all of it and publishes start to time out at the 20 s queue wait, 503).
- Severity: medium (authenticated CPU denial, needs only a circle of one's own). Before round 47 the same repeat was stopped at 20 a day.
- Fix: count refusals toward a second daily metric (for example 200 a day, or 20 free refusals then charge), or refuse a repeat of a refusal for the same head and decisions without stripping (cache the refusal for a minute by head id and decision hash). Giving back should apply to concurrency races and 5xx only, not to the work-doing 422s.

### B2. The evidence bound prunes versions an administrator's hold (or an open report) is protecting
- `server/worker/src/pool/circlesend.ts:157-181` (`evidenceBound`): its candidate query has no `NOT BLOCKING_HOLD_SQL(bank)` and no open-report clause. Every other prune path has both (`PRUNABLE_SQL` :130-134, the commit's prune :1016-1023, trim :186-192, D16: "never while the bank is held, never a version an open report names").
- Exploit (`c55/a2.test.ts`): author X sends 5 flagged versions in one day (the 5-a-day flag cap), the administrator places a manual hold `POST /admin/pool/circle-hold` to preserve them (200; the holds are `manual` and `blocklist`), the next day X sends a 6th matching version (allowed: the author may send into a held bank, 200). Version 1 goes to `status = 'pruned'`; `GET /admin/pool/circle-bank?id=..&version=1&basis=copyright` answers `404 version_not_retained, evidence_pruned: true`. The administrator's hold did not keep the body. The same omission applies to a version an open report names (same missing clause; I did not run that one).
- Severity: medium. The sender can erase the text the administrator is holding (anything beyond the flag's hashes and positions) with one send, after the admin's alert. Before round 47 a held bank was never pruned.
- Fix: add `AND NOT BLOCKING_HOLD_SQL(ev.bank_id) AND NOT EXISTS(open report on that version)` to the `evidenceBound` candidates (those versions then do not count as droppable, or count and are skipped), and let a hold lift the bound for that bank only up to a hard cap.

## Variants and limits

- V1. Evidence is displaced across senders. The bound ranks every flagged non-head version in the circle by commit time (5 a bank, 20 a circle, a quarter of `circle_bytes`; `circlesend.ts:164-174`), and an account may raise 5 flags a day per circle. An honest member whose version was flagged and who then sent a clean fix (so the flagged one is `previous`) can have the administrator's evidence pruned by someone else's later flagged sends: 4 days of one account (count rule), about 3 days with 4 MB sends (byte rule), minutes with 4 accounts. What remains is the flags' hashes and positions (`evidence_pruned`); the near-rewording false-positive review the evidence exists for is lost. The oldest-first rule destroys the oldest, i.e. the case the administrator is slowest on. Suggest: the bound prunes the sender's own newest evidence first, or evidence pruned this way is kept as long as its flag is younger than N days.
- V2. Pinned HEADS are outside the quarter-of-bytes rule (`x.status = 'previous'`, :174). One editor pins 5 banks a day (20 MB at 4 MB each) of flagged, withheld heads that neither trim nor purge-now can free (`evidence_kept` counts them). `c55/a3.test.ts` with `circle_bytes` scaled to 400 KB: 8 banks pinned 361 KB, every later new bank from the other members `413 quota_exceeded` from day 1; owner remedies worked only because 38 KB were free (the owner replaced a head with a small clean version, the old head became `previous` and the bound pruned it); with the circle full to the byte even that send is refused (the bound runs after the cap check), and the owner has to remove a clean bank to get room. At default sizes: 10 days for one account, 2.5 days for four, and the administrator's only means is resolving the flags. Much better than round 46 (under a day), and alerts are mailed (budgeted), so low-medium. Suggest: also bound heads (a flagged head beyond N per circle is pruned and the bank stays withheld with its flag's hashes), or let `commitVersion` count evidence-bound pruning as room (like PRUNABLE_SQL).
- V3. A version withheld past the flag cap (no flag): checked every path (`c55/a1.test.ts`, 8 matching sends by one author): owner, editor, author and viewer reads 404, rollback to it 400, history line blanked, a clean follow-up lifts the bank hold but not the version, `/admin/dmca/unblock {release_circle_versions}` releases the 5 flagged versions and none of the 3 flagless ones (candidates come from flags), the bank-wide `circle-release` leaves versions withheld, hide-off needs a `via: hide_key` flag, no other `withheld_at = NULL` exists. So it never becomes readable and the cap cannot be used to launder. Limits: (a) it stays withheld for good after the takedown is lifted (an honest author who iterated more than 5 times against a takedown that was later lifted has versions only a manual `circle-release {bank_id, version}` frees, and the only signal is one `cap` flag a day); (b) it is not evidence: the sender or owner can trim it away at once, so the sixth and later matching versions of a day are disposable by the sender; the administrator keeps `circle_qkeys` hashes and the cap flag.
- V4. Erasure leaves a row naming the user: `pool_alert_budget` channel `circle_email_domain:<hmac of domain>:<user id>` (`circlemail.ts:165`); `circleErasureStatements` deletes only `circle_email_inviter:<id>` (`circles.ts:1949`). `c55/i1.test.ts`: after `deleteContentStatements` the row remains until the cron's 48 h delete. The domain hash is keyed, so it says only "this id mailed some domain once in hour h", still a user id after erasure. Fix: `DELETE ... WHERE channel LIKE 'circle_email_domain:%:' || ?1` in both the erasure and the resweep.
- V5. Keyless guard cost: `circlesend.ts:615` validates the whole payload an extra time before `publishPrepare`. `c55/k1.test.ts`, 978 questions, 3.9 MB: `validate_for_publish` 108 ms, `publish_prepare` 163 ms (non-hiding), 669 ms (hiding). So a non-hiding send's share-core work is about 1.66x, a hiding one about 1.16x, and a payload that fails the first pass for another reason pays a second validation (`:768`) before it is refused. Heap: the returned tree did not stay resident (retained delta below noise); wasm memory peak is that of the prepare, so the reservation (`PUBLISH_COST_PER_BYTE` 16) still covers it. No bypass found: sentinel variants are not keyless by the app's own definition (exact string); `keylessPositions` reads share-core's error list, which is capped at 100 (`MAX_ERRORS`), but anything that can fill the cap with other errors (duplicate_choice, no_correct, placeholder_key) fails the real prepare as well, so a keyless question cannot hide behind truncation (placeholder_key is itself on the guard's list). Parts never carry the flag; images are not part of the rule. This remains a mistake-guard: an editor who deliberately blanks keys by removing the flag and sentinel can always do so (and a rollback restores).
- V6. `evidenceBound` runs in every commit and every effective PATCH; its plan (`c55/plan.test.ts`) is `SEARCH ev USING INDEX circle_versions_withheld (circle_id=?)` and then the row for `status`/`full_key` and an `admin_flags_publish` probe per row. Pruned withheld versions keep their rows forever (history lines), so the scan grows with every flagless withheld send (100 commits a day per account); after a few months a busy hostile circle reads thousands of rows per commit (D1 rows-read billing, latency). Suggest adding `status` to the partial index (`WHERE withheld_at IS NOT NULL AND status IN ('current','previous')` is not possible in a partial index on a mutable column, so index `(circle_id, status, committed_at)` filtered by `withheld_at IS NOT NULL AND full_key <> ''`) or keep a counter.
- V7. Hide-off restores a version the PATCHer's own exemption judges clean: the batch uses `COPYRIGHT_SQL(..., me)` with the PATCHer as sender (`circles.ts:1509-1511`), not the version's committer. Reachable only for a version that has a `via: hide_key` flag AND whose full form matches an active block for its committer but not for the PATCHer; a committed version that matched at send would carry a send flag and fail `ONLY_HIDE_FLAGS`, so I found no real path. Noting the parameter. Also hide-off resolves the administrator's flag as `resolved_by` the PATCHer (a member closing an admin queue item for its own `via: hide_key` flag); intended.
- V8. A stripped hidden read is refunded when `hold()` (the memory reservation after the strip, `circlehide.ts:226-238`) throws a 503: under memory contention a viewer can get free 150-200 ms strips. I could not trigger it in the harness (no contention); there is no other refundable error that costs a strip: versions without recorded decisions are refused before stripping (`circles.ts:1188`), recorded decisions strip deterministically, a missing body is a 404 before any strip. The 404/409 refunds are therefore not an oracle for the version's content (409 depends only on the stored `strip_opts`, which the viewer already learns from 409 vs 200).
- V9. Invite counters: `address_limit` is still answered before anything is charged (unmetered probe of "has this mailbox had 3 today", by any owner; known from 54b V4). The new `429 circle_email_domain_hourly` tells a caller that colleagues recently mailed a provider (no who or where). The domain's last fifth for light inviters (3 mails) can be consumed by 10 approved sybil accounts at 3 mails each (a domain closes for an hour; needs 10 approved accounts). A masked label's ` #n` suffix is computed over all email invites of the circle, expired and revoked ones included, so it counts old invites too; seen by the owner only. The 3 mails/day per mailbox still silently suppresses (`202` never shown, now `422 invite_not_sent`, good).
- V10. `purge-now` that answers `409 pending_review` has already claimed the purge and pruned (`circles.ts:1823-1850`). What it deletes: every `current`/`previous` version without an open send flag, including flagless withheld ones, and their blob rows; it keeps flagged withheld ones (verified by `c54/t4` and `c55/a3`: `evidence_kept`), so the owner cannot destroy anything an open flag names, and the claim is guarded (`removed_at IS NOT NULL`, owner, no blocking hold or open report, `changes() = 1` on the prune). It cannot be undone (documented). A 409 that did irreversible work is a product hazard: the app that reads any 4xx as "nothing changed" will mislead the owner. Consider 202.
- V11. Held-publish budget: a hide-on PATCH charges the PATCHer's `held_publishes` for matches in OLD versions an editor sent (`circles.ts:1706`), and the flags it raises carry the PATCHer's `user_id` (and count against the PATCHer's 5 flags). An editor can seed a bank with labelled copies of a taken-down text (the labelled form does not match at send), and the author's later hide-on then withholds them with her own name and spend. Needs 20 positions to reach the cap alert; the alert only informs the administrator.

## Checked and held (no finding)

- Flag cap: `FLAG_ROOM_SQL` counts by `user_id` and `circle_id` since UTC midnight, inside the same batch as the insert (D1 batch is serial), kinds both counted, shared by sends, rollbacks and PATCH; the 6th matching send in a day is `withheld: true, held: true`, no flag, one `cap` alert. Another sender's cap is independent (an attacker cannot spend an honest author's 5).
- Evidence bound authz: runs only in batches already guarded by `won`; never prunes a head; never touches an unflagged or a readable version; flags updated only for versions pruned in that very statement time (a window prune in the same millisecond can enter the same set but those have no open flags).
- Unblock release: guarded by the admin route; per candidate the body is re-scanned as a send (student data exact and near, every active block, committer's exemptions), then re-checked inside the batch (`COPYRIGHT_ONLY_SQL`: no student-data match, no blocking hold, no open report, every open flag is a `blocklist` flag with all takedowns lifted). `c54/rel.test.ts`: the student-data-after-send case now releases 0. A missing or unreadable body is skipped.
- Hide-off undoes only what a `via: hide_key` flag withheld, only when no other open flag exists, re-checked in the batch; admin/report holds are untouched (`circle_holds` sources outside `SEND_HOLD_SOURCES`); `c54/t2` shows restore and re-withhold.
- PATCH counters: `giveDaily` clamps at 0 (`shared.ts:592`), gives back once per charge, never from a changed 200; the day-boundary case gives at most one extra change. No replay path bypasses the 20 cap (but see B1).
- Email invites: address folding and the cap (c53 `e1`, c54 `m1` re-run), per-inviter hourly and domain shares (c54 `t1`: 10 of one inviter, 5 more refused `429 hourly_limit`, a small inviter gets in), open email invites counted apart from plain codes (`OPEN_INVITES_SQL` kind), masked label for one-character local parts hidden, refund on 409/404 returns counters only (no mail on failure).
- Migration 0114 is one partial index, no data change (fresh DB and the suite apply it); 0111-0113 as in 54b. Public publish path: `invalid` export and `privacyScan` signature are circle-only, the public tests (652) pass; phases A/B tests unchanged except the intended edits.

## Nitpicks

- `purge-now`'s 409 message says "Everything else in it has been deleted now": true, but not that the bank cannot be restored; the README says so only in passing.
- `circle_send_flags` `cap` alert gives the administrator no list of the flagless versions (they are findable only by the bank hold). Include bank id and version count.
- `UNBLOCK_RELEASE_MAX` 50: the 51st+ candidate keeps `block_lifted` and is released only by a second unblock call or `circle-release`; the response says only the counts.
- `trim`'s `pinned_versions` is still `[]` while `evidence_kept` is 5 (the number is said, which fixes the earlier nitpick; the versions are not).
