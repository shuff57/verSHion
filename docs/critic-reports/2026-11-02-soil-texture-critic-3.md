# Soil texture triangle, critic 3 (accuracy + teacher + regression), commit b6e99c6

Verdict: FAIL. 0 blocker, 0 major, 3 MED, 5 LOW, 4 minor.
(The critic could not write its report file; condensed from its final answer. Tools, probe, mutants and PNGs: scratchpad/soil-crit3/.)

## MED 1: a callout prints a class name inside a different class, sometimes right beside the sample, and in Print the leader disappears into the hatch
The round-3 callout puts a label whose own region has no clear place in the nearest clear place in another region, joined by a 1.1 px leader and a 2.2 px dot that are almost the same weight and colour as a class boundary (#111 vs #222, both 1.1 px). One sample, Large, guides on, full names: sand 8 / clay 54 (Clay) prints "SiC" about 9 px from the marker inside the Clay region on a 17 px leader to Silty clay (png/m854-stu.png); a student reads SiC beside the dot though the answer is clay. Sweep every 1 % at Large: 309 of 5,151 renders (6 %) have a callout; all 363 callouts are SiC inside Clay (185) or SiCL inside Silt loam (170); in 11 renders the foreign label is under 30 px from a sample in the host class (sand 8-9, clay 51-59), in 23 under 60 px, in 80 inside the key's answer outline. Two samples: sand 11 / clay 52 with 35 / 10 at Large prints "Silty clay loam" on two lines inside Clay and inside the key's Clay outline under sample 1, which is Clay (p2-Large-11-52-35-10.png); 25/25 with 26/32 puts "SiC" inside Silt loam on a 134 px leader crossing all of SiCL; fenced cases land "Silty clay" or "SiC" in Clay next to the "Clay" label. 19 % of random pairs at Large get a callout (582/3000), Medium 1.7 %, Small 0.3 %. Print at 66 % grey: the SiC leader vanishes into the vertical hatch and "SiC" floats in the dotted Clay area beside the marker (zoom-m854-bw66.png); the SiCL dot is barely visible in the cross-hatch. The FAQ calls this "a clear place nearby with a short leader" and never says the name lands in another class.
Fix: never print a class name inside another class; put it below or outside the triangle on a leader like S, LS, Si, or keep it in its own region with the guide broken under its white plate (the textbook way). At minimum exclude host places within about 60 px of a sample and never use a sample's class or the answer region as host; give leaders and dots a white edge in Print; add a test: no label text inside a region it does not name.

## MED 2: number tags can sit far from their marker, and nearer the other one
The ring search out to 90 px is new this round. Sand 8 / clay 7 with 2 / 2 at Large (both Silt, about 6 % apart): tag "2" is 70 px from marker 2 and right above marker 1 (p2-Large-8-7-2-2.png). Sand 3 / clay 97 with 57 / 38: tag "1" floats 74 px below its marker in the middle of Clay. 9,456 two-sample renders: a tag nearer the other marker in 12/3000 random pairs at Large, 2/1500 Medium, 2/1500 Small, and 88/778 (11 %) for pairs within 4 % at Large; 232 renders have a tag more than 25 px from its marker (worst 74 px). Mutant T1 (ring capped at 30 px) and C10 (12 px tag-to-class-name margin removed) survive the full suite; no test pins tag nearness.
Fix: cap tag distance at about marker radius + tag diagonal + 12 px; beyond that draw a leader from the tag to its marker; assert each tag is nearer its own marker.

## MED 3: draw time, the callout search freezes the editor for seconds
Single-threaded, warm cache, load 23-33 on 24 cores. Two samples 16/51 + 7/71 Large: 2.6 s release-like (as shipped: opt-level "z", LTO off, 16 codegen units), 16.8 s debug; 8/48 + 19/42: 2.6 s / 7.1 s; four other pairs 1.1-1.9 s / 5.4-7.2 s; one sample sand 11 clay 34-40 Large 0.41-0.58 s / 2.2-5.6 s; default first draw 70 ms. In the loaded 6-thread debug sweep 143 two-sample renders took over 5 s and 128 one-sample renders over 1 s. Every keystroke draws again (the cache matches identical params only), so typing a second silty sample at Large hangs the editor; the wasm build will be slower. The builder's speed test draws only sand 30-59 / clay 20 and never reaches a callout. Cause: `callout()` collects every 3 px candidate in eleven regions for each label variant and each of 8 dots, sorts them, and on failure repeats everything in the crossing pass.
Fix: prune candidates by leader length before sorting, stop at the first variant that succeeds, add a timing test on the configs above.
