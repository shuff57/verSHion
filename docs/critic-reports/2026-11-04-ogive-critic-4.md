## MED
1. **Three- and four-column pastes are still read as lower/upper/frequency, and the advice chain settles on invented classes.** `1,000, 2,000, 5, 8 / 2,000, 3,000, 7, 15` (lower, upper, frequency, cumulative) offers `"1, 0, 2"`, then `"0, 1, 2"`, then merges to `0 to 1 f2 / 1 to 2 f3` — total 5 instead of 12, both classes wrong. Cause: `cells()` (ogive.rs:291-303) uses the thousands-stripped cells only when they are 2 or 3 long, so a fourth column discards the correct 4 cells for the raw 6-cell comma split; the right numbers are in hand and thrown away. `midpoint, f, cf / 1,000, 3, 3 / 2,000, 5, 8` (a header naming the columns) settles at `3 to 1000 f3 / 1000 to 2000 f8`: the header is skipped and never used to pick the midpoint reading. Claim 3 holds for 2 and 3 cells only.
