# Soil texture triangle, critic 4 (accuracy + teacher + regression), commit 411cf74

Verdict: FAIL. 0 blocker, 0 major, 3 MED, 5 LOW, 3 minor.
(The critic could not write its report file; condensed from its final answer. Probe tools/crit4.rs, PNGs out/png, mutant logs mut/: scratchpad/soil-crit4/.)

52,277 renders analysed: one sample 38,658 (every 1 % of sand and clay, 3 sizes, guides, abbreviations at Medium and Large, guides off, student copies, Print every 2 %), silt typed 1,326, three typed 1,498, two samples 10,795 (5,500 random pairs, 3,300 pairs within 4 %, 1,395 vertex/edge/corner/on-line pairs, 600 close pairs as abbreviations/student copies/Print).

## MED 1: a sample can be left without a number tag, and nothing says so
When all four tag stages fail the code stores no tag and moves on; there is no fallback and no editor note. 116 of 10,795 two-sample renders (1.1 %) have two markers and one tag: 68 of 2,000 pairs within 4 % at Large (3.4 %), 21 of 800 at Medium, 5 of 500 at Small, 7 of 3,000 random pairs at Large; 112 of the 116 have no editor note. Repro: sand 94 clay 2 with sand 90 clay 6 at Large (sample 1 has no tag); sand 52 clay 45 with 53/46 at Medium (two touching markers, a single "1"); sand 99/1 with 100/0 fails at every size. The FAQ says each marker gets a number tag; the committed tests check `tags >= 2` only on their own point lists, which never hit this.
Fix: a last stage that always places a tag (outside the triangle on a leader, or the nearest ring place on an opaque plate); if even that fails an editor note; assert two tags (or "1, 2") over a dense sweep of close pairs and corners.

## MED 2: a class can still be missing from the triangle, and it is always the answer's class
82 of 10,795 two-sample renders (0.76 %, all at Large) leave Silty clay (37), Sandy clay (34) or Silty clay loam (11) unnamed; in all 82 the missing class is one sample's answer. The builder's "73 renders with both samples in it" is wrong both ways: 36 of 3,000 random pairs at Large (1.2 %) are affected, often with only one sample in the region. Repro: sand 56 clay 41 (sandy clay) with sand 29 clay 20 at Large: the sandy clay triangle has no label (png/tr-Large-56-41-29-20.png). The note says "see the legend" but the legend cannot show which region is sandy clay, so a student cannot classify that sample. Likely cause: in the gap pass the third flag in `tries` (keep the 12 px tag margin) is always `true`; a relaxed try was evidently intended and never written.
Fix: before giving up retry the smaller abbreviation with the tag margin cut to about 4 px and the marker margin cut to its white ring, then relax the 14 px end rule for the sample end only; assert no missing label over random pairs at Large. One sample is clean (0 missing in 38,658).

## MED 3: tags almost as near the other marker as their own (critic 3's MED 2, one step over)
The required margin is the smaller of 2 x marker radius + 6 px and half the distance between the markers, so for close pairs it shrinks to 1-3 px. 227 tags in 126 renders sit within 3 px of equidistant from both markers with no leader: 70 of 2,000 close pairs at Large plus cases at Medium, Small, Print, student copies and 7 random pairs. Repro: sand 78 clay 16 with 82/17 at Large: "2" is 22.9 px from its own marker and 24.2 px from the other; also 95/4 with 87/10 and 92/8 with 93/5 at Large (30.9 vs 32.0 px). The committed tests check only "strictly nearer"; mutant M11 (margin removed entirely) survives the whole suite.
Fix: require the tag on the far side of its own marker (more than about 100 degrees from the direction of the other marker) or nearer by at least marker radius + 4 px, otherwise use a leader; test the margin.

## LOW
1. Gaps can be long: with two samples at Large a plate along a horizontal clay line hides up to 51.8 px (sand 83 clay 2 with 4/38: "SiCL" on sample 2's clay line); another hides 49 % of a short guide (50/39 with 53/43, Print). Lines are still traceable; one sample is milder (at most 18.2 px, 25 % of a guide). Fix: count hidden length extra for plates parallel to a line and cap near 30 px before trying the smaller abbreviation.
2. The gap can start right after the dot: the 14 px end distance is measured from the sample's centre, so at Large only about 7 px (one dash) shows past the white ring; closest plate 14.1 px from the centre; in the Print render of 8/54 a stub then "SiCL" follows the dot (png/z-gP.png), weak in a 66 % grey copy. Fix: measure from the ring edge plus about 12 px.
3. Tags over a line are more common than claimed: 320 of 10,795 renders (3.0 %), 50 of 3,000 random pairs at Large (builder said 2.3 %, almost all within 4 %); they look acceptable (opaque plate, readable). Mutant M12 (an over-line tag may stand beyond the cap with no leader) survives because no test reaches that stage.
4. Draw time: the critic-3 configurations are fixed; worst now close pairs in the sandy loam / sandy clay loam band at Large (61/28 with 64/29, 65/22 with 62/18, 57/29 with 70/24): 60-85 ms release-like (single thread, load 60-80), 330-450 ms debug; worst placement work 410,932 units vs the committed test's worst 235k; the 1M bound lets a 4x slowdown through (mutant M19, four repeats of every clear-pass box test, survives). Fix: add 61/28 + 64/29 to the work test and set the bound near 1.5x the worst.
5. The census protects none of the code changed this round: census_baseline passes and "neither hash changed" is plausible (the fixture hashes only the defaults and the opening state, one sample at Medium, which never need a gap (0 in 15,453) or tags). Fix: add a Large two-sample case and a Large gap case.

## minor
