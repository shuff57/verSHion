# Figure 1.3 (right-triangle-trig), combined critic on round 2 (commit 0f81c30)

Blind combined critic (accuracy + teacher + regression in one pass), run 2026-10-27 in a
`git archive 0f81c30` copy at `/tmp/critic13` with its own target dir, full suite at `-j2`.
Scope agreed with the owner: the critic judges only the must-do list plus accuracy and leaks;
everything else is a minor or backlog.

## Verdict: FAIL (one major)

"The triangle, the Given/Ask/Hidden blanks and the answer key are right, but if I set Decimal
places to 0 the key prints a 5-9-10 'right' triangle and 'cos θ = 9/10 = 0.87', so the sheet
contradicts itself and I would not hand it out until that rounding is fixed."

## Major

**The printed fraction is not the ratio of the printed sides, and at 0 decimal places the labelled
sides are no longer a right triangle.** `Ctx::n` honours `dec` for the side labels and the fraction
operands while the decimal is worked out from the exact sides, so the three disagree at low
precision.

- `{"define":"Angle and hypotenuse","theta":30,"hyp_len":10}`, `dec = 0`: `solve` gives 5 / 8.660254
  / 10; the key labels read 5, 9, 10 (5²+9²=106≠100, under a drawn right angle) and the readout
  reads `cos θ = 9/10 = 0.87` and `tan θ = 5/9 = 0.58`. The student copy with the sides Given shows
  the same lines.
- `{"define":"Angle and adjacent","theta":35,"adj_len":12}`, `dec = 0`: `sin θ = 8/15 = 0.57` with
  labels 8 / 12 / 15.
- `{"define":"Two legs","opp_len":8.4025,"adj_len":12}`, `dec = 0`, hint on:
  `θ = arctan(8/12) = 35°`, but arctan(8/12) = 33.69°.
- At 4 dp with a long operand: `5/8.6603 = 0.5774` while 5/8.6603 = 0.57735.

Accepted fix (round 3): lengths and fraction operands print with at least two decimals
(`dec.max(2)`), the printed decimal is worked out from the printed operands, theta keeps `dec`.

## Minors (do not fail the figure)

1. A Hidden side still prints its name (`hypotenuse` with no value), while the FAQ says "Hidden is
   not shown"; behaviour and wording disagree. Wording fixed in round 3.
2. The FAQ promises the key shows the inverse trig, but `arctan(...)` only appears while the SOH
   hint is on; it also said "35 degrees to one decimal place" where the key prints `35°`.
3. The 22 px arc-radius floor does not touch the 10° case (there the radius is 38 px, its maximum);
   what fixed 10° is the new placement. The progress doc's "about 4 px of chord" at 10° is wrong:
   it measures about 6.6 px. Doc corrected in round 3.
4. At 10–20° the θ label sits about 2 px below the base immediately left of `adjacent = ... cm`
   and reads as one line with it; the leader is suppressed by the 3 px rule.
5. Dragging the far vertex to the top can trip the workspace's 0.1 snapping and raise "The angle
   was kept between 10 and 80 degrees" although the teacher only dragged. The drag tests call the
   closure without `normalize(snap = true)`, which the editor does call. Backlog.
6. `docs/figures-progress.md` line 111 said "Five examples" and "51 new ... files only" while round
   2 added a sixth example and a 52nd census file. Corrected in round 3.
7. The readout blank renders `θ = _____ °` with a visible gap before the degree sign.
8. With Large labels the `adjacent = ...` label is pushed far below the base, close to the readout
   rule.

## Backlog (out of scope)

- The picture is to scale in shape and `solve` ignores the Given/Ask/Hidden states, so theta and the
  ratios are measurable from the drawing in every state; only the absolute scale is free. Inherent
  to the required marked-angle diagram, not the state-dependent leak that was fixed. A "not to
  scale" note would be the only escape.
- Radians; a key-only worked method line; the other acute angle as 90 − θ; a size handle.
- Derived lengths can exceed the input maximum (Angle and opposite, θ=80, opp=10000 → hyp 10154).
- Offering Decimal places 0/1 at all for a trig figure.
