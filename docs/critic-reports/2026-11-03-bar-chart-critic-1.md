## MAJOR
J1. Thousands separators are silently read as a second group: a wrong chart with no note. `Walk, 1,200 / Car, 2,500 / Bus, 3,000` draws "Group 1 / Group 2" with Walk 1/200, Car 2/500, Bus 3/0 and no note. `Walk 1,200 / Car 950` (the "Red 12" form) gives a category "Walk 1" with count 200. The figure prints its own counts with thousands commas (fmt_count), so retyping them gives a wrong chart. Mixed lines (`Walk, 1,200 / Car, 950`) are refused with "change line 2 to “Car, 950, 0”" and applying it settles on a wrong two-group chart with no note. Decimal comma (`Walk; 12,5`) also silently becomes two groups. Fix: read `\d{1,3}(,\d{3})+` as one count or refuse with "write 1200 without the comma"; never advise padding with ", 0" there.
