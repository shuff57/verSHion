## MINOR 1 (pre-existing in 48d7701; the neighbour of round 11's fix): Expected cells of 13-15 digits are still wrong in the last digit

- Files:
  - `chisquarefit.rs:203`: E = r / R · N in f64, with N an f64 sum of the counts.
  - `:463`: `exp_full` lets any cell with ≤ 15 printed digits print in full.
  - `:464`: `fmt_exp`.
- The round-11 rule says a cell "prints in full only while every cell needs <= 15 digits and is exactly representable".
  That holds for the typed Observed cells. The Expected cells are computed. With rounding error in the f64 sum, the
  division and the multiplication (several ulp, about 3-9e-16 relative), a 15-digit cell can land on the wrong side of
  the last printed digit. So can a 14- or 13-digit cell when the exact value sits near a rounding boundary.
- Repros. All plain, all wrong by more than half a unit, and all byte-identical to 48d7701:
  - `{"obs":"7071192070493, 7906420125171, 4655731653498","ratio":"0.3, 0.5, 0.3","dec":2}` → `P/r11c-pngs/e15_a.png`
    - Expected (row 2) prints `8,924,247,204,164.54`.
    - Exact: 19,633,343,849,162 × 0.5 / 1.1 = 8,924,247,204,164.5454…, which rounds to `.55`.
  - `{"obs":"2775961094907.38, 7798100695172.28, 7252027483271.12, 6200853972373.16","ratio":"9, 7, 2, 11","dec":2}`
    → `P/r11c-pngs/e15_c.png`, and at Small `P/r11c-pngs/x_e15_c_S.png`
    - Row 1 prints `7,456,637,559,017.78`. Exact: 24,026,943,245,723.94 × 9/29 = …017.7745, which rounds to `.77`.
  - `{"obs":"84991091988.1696, 39797894800.1575, 79281869152.9191, 56268252141.2311","ratio":"7, 3, 3, 3","dec":3}`
    → `P/r11c-pngs/e14_a.png` (14 digits)
    - `48,813,582,765.465` three times. Exact: 260,339,108,082.4773 × 3/16 = 48,813,582,765.46449375, which rounds to `.464`.
  - `{"obs":"7483764344.997, 6965989825.9973, 5914795813.3518, 7078515657.6965, 5949590632.9005, 2390874591.7062","ratio":"7, 0.7, 11, 9, 13, 13","dec":3}`
    → `P/r11c-pngs/e13_a.png` (13 digits)
    - Row 3 prints `7,329,959,767.842`. Exact: 35,783,530,866.6493 × 11 / 53.7 = 7,329,959,767.8425009…, which rounds to `.843`.
- Rate:
  - A 1,500-page sweep of plain columns at 1e8-1e14 through the real renderer gave 5 pages with a wrong cell, all 15-digit.
  - An f64 simulation of 325,783 plain-Expected tables gave 2,819 wrong cells: 2,628 at 15 digits, 177 at 14, 14 at 13.
  - The random 6,909-page sweep gave 4 (all 15-digit). 48d7701 had 1,521 E-cell errors, mostly 16+ digits, so this is a
    large improvement.
- Why the new test misses it: `every_printed_number_derives_from_the_counts_at_six_decimals` (`:1467`) allows
  `e.abs() * 2e-15` on Expected cells. At 1e13 that is 0.02, four times the half-unit of 0.005, so it cannot see this.
- Graded minor, following the tenth report's precedent for this class. It needs counts of about 1e9 or more with
  decimals and is not new. By the letter of "a number not derivable from the printed givens" it is a value error; upgrade
  it if the loop applies the letter.
- Fix direction: compute E exactly from the i128 units and the ratio, as the Observed total already is, and round it to
  `dec`. Or print in full only while the cell has at most about 12 significant digits.

## Verified correct

Rounding (`exact_six`), each rendered and looked at:

- Half-up at the boundary:
  - `0.0000005` → 0.000001 (`r_half_0000005`).
  - `0.0000015` → 0.000002 and `0.0000025` → 0.000003 (`r_0000015`).
  - `1.2345675` → 1.234568 and `2.2345665` → 2.234567 (`r_1_2345675`).
  - `1e-7` → 0, `5e-7` → 0.000001, `1.5e-6` → 0.000002 (`r_1e-7_5e-7`).
  - `0.1234565` → 0.123457 (`r_0_1234565`).
  - `0.49999999999999999999e-6` → 0 and `0.50000000000000000001e-6` → 0.000001 (sweep).
  - `9999999.9999995` → 10,000,000.
- Exponent forms: `1E3`, `1e+3`, `1e+0003`, `.5`, `5.`, `+3`, `.5e1`, `1.e2` (`r_1E3`).
- Leading zeros: `0001.50`, `0007` (`r_lead0`).
- A 40-decimal count: `1234567.8901234567890123456789012345678901` → 1,234,567.890123 (`r_long40`).
- Out of range:
  - `1e-400` → 0 and `4.9e-324` → 0, with the note.
  - `1e400`, `NaN`, `inf` → "not counts" (`r_1e400`, `r_nan`).
- Negatives:
  - `-0.0000005` is ignored as not a count (`r_neg5e7`).
  - `-0` and `-0.0` → 0, with no note.
- Rejected forms: `1 000`, `5 peas`, `0x10`, `1_000`, `1e`, `.e1` are all reported as not counts.
- Separators:
  - `1,000` splits into 1 and 000. This is the long-standing comma-separated list format, the same in 48d7701.
  - Empty tokens and `;` are handled (`r_empty`).
