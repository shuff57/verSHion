Options and bounds:
mode: Arithmetic (first + difference), Geometric (first and ratio), Explicit f(n), Recursive with two seeds.
start: 0 or 1; count: 3–24 terms.
display: Terms, Partial sums, Both. Two aligned graphs always use Both; Number line plots term positions on a shared value axis, with stems marking stacked dots.
marks: Dots or Spikes; join adds light connecting lines.
from/to: lower and upper vertical bounds (number-line horizontal bounds), within -100 to 100; step: 0.000001–20; all three axis controls use 0.000001 increments.
grid: None, Light, Standard, Bold.
limit: draw an established finite limit; this control is absent where the analysis cannot establish one. band: L ± epsilon; epsilon is a typed positive measure.
point_values: collision-checked numeric labels beside their own marks; terms/sums: lists below the graph.
explicit/recursive: written formula rows; sum: standard finite-sum formula, computed finite sum, and geometric infinity sum when established; convergence: classification and established limit, otherwise cannot determine.
xlabel/ylabel: names at arrow tips; over-wide names and names clashing with sequence symbols are refused with a panel note.
title: typed caption; blank_title: blank line. Student copies always use a blank title to prevent answer leakage.
task: Find formula, Missing terms (comma-separated indices), Continue (first given terms, from 1–6; always leave at least one term to continue).
Classic examples: Arithmetic, Geometric halving, Alternating halves, Fibonacci, Reciprocals, Geometric partial sums.
show_answers — standard student/key toggle.
style — Colour or grey-only Print.
label_size — Small, Medium, Large; scales labels and expands the canvas.

Mode relevance: first is absent in Explicit; difference/ratio only Arithmetic/Geometric; formula only Explicit; recurrence and second seed only Recursive. Aligned graphs always show both series; display is hidden there. Number line hides grid, step, point values, and band controls. Student copies hide title/limit controls; missing/given controls appear only for their student task.
Typed inputs accept arithmetic, fractions, decimals, scientific notation, roots and pi via the shared reader; expressions use the existing evaluator where exact arithmetic cannot establish a value. Rational arithmetic propagates exactness; it never recognises floating values as symbolic forms.
The recursive formula uses both initial values (first at the selected start index, second at the next index), then a_(n-1) and a_(n-2).
Interpretation of epsilon: distance epsilon either side of L, i.e. L-epsilon < y < L+epsilon.
An unestablished limit, recurrence or explicit form is reported as cannot determine. General symbolic limit solving is intentionally not claimed.
